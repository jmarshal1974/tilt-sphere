WELCOME TO THE MOVEMENT LAB

A little tilt.
A lot of momentum.

Use WASD or arrow keys to tilt. Release to level the board; the sphere keeps rolling. Tilt the opposite way to slow down. C switches between course and cabin view. Drag the scene in cabin view to look around. Space pauses.

Two-hand slope · VR

Equal-height hands level the board. Raise one hand and the slope runs toward the lower hand. Stagger your hands forward/backward to steer along that axis. Keep the controllers separated.

Independent knobs · VR

Hold both grip buttons. Rotate the left controller nose down/up for forward/back tilt; roll the right controller for sideways tilt. Each grip captures a neutral orientation when pressed. Release either grip to level the board.

Headset controls

Left X: switch input experiment. Left stick click: recenter. Right A: reset ball. Right B: pause. The cabin follows travel direction, while you can still look freely.

Reach the mint goal ring and settle below 0.6 m/s. Holes reset the ball after a short fade. The copper ramps and brown mud patch are optional experiments. Tune the controls before entering VR. Headset feel is not yet verified.
